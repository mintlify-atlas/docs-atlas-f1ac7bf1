## Why Fula Storage?

Fula Storage combines the familiarity of the S3 API with the resilience of decentralized storage and the privacy of end-to-end encryption — all without changing how you write code.

## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a JWT token">
    Authenticate with your Functionland account at `https://auth.fx.land` to receive a JWT access token. See the [Authentication guide](/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your S3 client">
    Set your endpoint to `https://s3.cloud.fx.land` and use `JWT:<your-token>` as the access key ID. Any S3-compatible tool works without modification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a bucket and upload">
    Use your existing S3 tooling to create buckets and upload objects. For private data, use the [Encrypted Client SDK](/guides/encryption) to encrypt files before they leave your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Raw S3 tools (AWS CLI, boto3) upload plaintext data that gateway operators can read. For private files, use the [Encrypted Client SDK](/guides/encryption) to ensure client-side encryption.
</Note>
